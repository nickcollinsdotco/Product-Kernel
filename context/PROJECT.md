# Product Kernel

## What is this?

Product Kernel is a meta-context framework for AI-native product
design and development.

It combines structured product reasoning, persistent project context,
autonomous implementation, and human validation.

## Why does it exist?

AI coding agents are increasingly capable of producing software,
but they do not automatically possess durable understanding of:

- product intent
- user needs
- conceptual models
- previous decisions
- project constraints
- tradeoffs
- unfinished work

Product Kernel treats this knowledge as project infrastructure.

## Core thesis

An autonomous agent needs more than instructions.

It needs a persistent model of the product it is operating within.

## Primary influences

- Layers by Jamie Mill
- Archē by Josh Millgate

## Status

Experimental / evolving.

## Current objective

Develop a practical, reusable methodology for AI-assisted product
design and development that can eventually be packaged as Claude
skills, commands, templates, and examples.
